External Attack Surface Management

A multi-client platform that continuously maps what an organisation exposes to the internet, and what of it is actually exploitable.

ReactFastAPIPythonNIST NVDCISA KEV

The problem

You cannot defend what you cannot see. Organisations accumulate forgotten subdomains, exposed services, and expired certificates faster than anyone tracks them, and a scanner that dumps thousands of findings without priority is just a different kind of blindness. The question is not what is exposed, it is what is exposed and exploitable right now.

What I built

A multi-client platform that continuously discovers an organisation's external footprint, subdomains, exposed IPs, open ports, running services, and TLS certificates, and correlates every finding against live NIST NVD and CISA KEV data to surface prioritised, exploitable vulnerabilities in real time.

  • An analytics dashboard with CVE trend visualisation and real-time scan-job monitoring, so a SOC team has an always-on view of each client's attack surface.
  • Bulk vulnerability status workflows, so triage scales past the single-finding click.
  • Prioritisation by exploited status, not raw severity, which is what pulls mean time to remediation down.

The result

The platform turns an unbounded list of exposures into a short, ranked list of things worth fixing first. That ranking is the product, discovery is table stakes, and deciding what to do with it is where the value sits.
